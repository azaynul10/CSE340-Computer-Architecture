## Advice that recurs in every senior's post

- **Finish each week's material in that week.** Every "CSE340 is brutal" story traces back to cramming.
- **Raw marks.** Mid 25 raw, Final 50 → 40. Losing 3 marks on the mid is 3 marks off your grade — there is no scaling to hide behind.
- **Practice, don't watch.** Slides + PBK handouts + practice sheets, then blind timed reproduction. Reading a solution ≠ being able to produce it in 90 minutes closed-book.
- **Go to consultation.** Faculty repeat exam patterns in consultation hours far more than in lectures.
- **Don't give up mid-semester.** Best-3-of-4 quizzes means one bad quiz is erased; a 54/55 final after a 0/15 quiz has happened.
